# Endgame: The Ad Astra Rift & The Final Showdown

The climax of *Attack of the Mutants* hinges on control of the **Ad Astra Lab** and the activation of Dr. Rosemarie Mincey’s experimental space-time rift[cite: 3, 5].

---

## 1. Triggering the Endgame

The game timeline runs for a standard 10 turns, culminating in either a Turn 10 or Turn 11 final showdown depending on Dr. Mincey's research progress[cite: 3, 5]:

* **Early Activation (End of Turn 10 Showdown):** If Dr. Mincey occupies the Ad Astra Lab continuously from **Turn 6 through Turn 9**, she accelerates the field harmonics[cite: 3, 5]. The portal opens early, triggering the Final Confrontation during **Turn 10**[cite: 3].
* **Standard Activation (Turn 11 Showdown):** If Dr. Mincey was interrupted, relocated, or absent during those turns, the portal powers up on its standard baseline cycle[cite: 3, 5]. The humans must survive the siege through Turn 10; holding the lab triggers the Final Confrontation during **Turn 11**[cite: 5].

---

## 2. Immediate Mutant Victory Condition

* **Mechanism Destroyed:** If at any point the mutants obtain **sole control of the Ad Astra Lab** with no living humans or functional robots present, they immediately trash the equipment and smash the mechanism[cite: 3]. 
* **Result:** The time machine is destroyed, the mutation wave becomes permanent, and the **Mutants win the game immediately**[cite: 3, 5]. This is true **even if Julian Manbender is killed**.

---

## 3. The Time Rift Transition

If the Human player maintains control of the Ad Astra Lab at the trigger point (End of Turn 9 or Turn 10)[cite: 3, 5]:

1. **Who Travels:** Every human and robot physically present inside the The Tech Room steps through the portal[cite: 3]. Any humans or bots left in other rooms or corridors are left behind in the overrun campus[cite: 3].
2. **Destination:** The strike team emerges in the past inside **Dr. Julian Manbender's Private Research Lab**[cite: 3, 5]—catching him just as he prepares the mutagenic cocktail before the Green Energy concert[cite: 5].

---

## 4. The Final Confrontation Rules

The battle inside Manbender’s lab is fought using the Tactical Combat Pad with special scenario modifications[cite: 3, 5]:

### Forces Present
* **Human Strike Team:** All surviving humans and robots that were inside the Ad Astra Lab when the portal opened[cite: 3].
* **Mutant Defenders:** **Dr. Julian Manbender** (`CV: 4`, grants `+1 CV` on defense to mutants sharing his area) and his **5 Elite Campus Security Mutants** (`CV: 5` each)[cite: 3, 5]. If the Ad Astra Lab produced a result at the start of turn 4 then only 3 Campus Security Guard Mutants will be present.

### Battle Modifications
* **Surprise Round:** Because the human assault team materializes unexpectedly out of thin air, all mutant defenders suffer **-1 CV to defense during Round 1**[cite: 3].
* **No Round Limit:** This engagement is **not** limited to the standard two-round combat rule[cite: 3]. Rounds continue sequentially until one side is completely eliminated or the objective is met[cite: 3].
* **No Wounded Drag-Off:** The chaotic, enclosed lab environment prevents casualty extraction[cite: 3]. Human casualties cannot be dragged into safety; once downed, they remain in the Wounded box for the duration[cite: 3].
* **No Reserves In Retreat:** There are no adjacent safe rooms to fall back to—this is a fight to the bitter end[cite: 3].

---

## 5. Ultimate Victory & Defeat

* **Human Victory:** If **Dr. Julian Manbender is killed**, the human strike team wins[cite: 3]! With the mad mastermind eliminated before the concert detonation, the timeline collapses, the mutagen is never released, and history is restored[cite: 3, 5]. You can keep playing to see if any human's survive fighting the remaining Campus Security Mutants, just to have an ending to the story, but even if they don't survive, so long as Doctor Manbender has been eliminated, the game is a Human win.
* **Mutant Victory:** If all human strike team members are eliminated and **Dr. Manbender is still alive**, the **Mutants win**[cite: 3]. Manbender finishes his concoction unhindered, secures his thralls, and establishes his mutant utopia[cite: 3, 5].
