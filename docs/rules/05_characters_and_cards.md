## Event Card Deck

Draw 1 card during Phase 1[cite: 3, 5]. The effect lasts **only for the current turn** and only applies if the named character is currently in play[cite: 3, 5].

### Professor Applewhite Deck
* **"Maybe they can be reasoned with!"** — Mutants gain `+1 CV` in the first combat this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Save your ammo, bullets can’t hurt them!"** — Humans suffer `-1 CV` in the first combat this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Robots should not have military use."** — GripBots™ may drag up to 2 wounded elements during retreats this turn[cite: 6]. Elements are humans or bots.
* **"If my calculations are right…"** — GripBots™ gain `+1 CV` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Grant money will flow if we survive!"** — Optionally fight 1 additional combat round in an engagement this turn[cite: 6].

### Professor Richardson Deck
* **"Here’s your education…"** — All robots gain `+1 CV` in their next fight this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Dance, Mutant, Dance!"** — All GunBots™ roll `3x3` CV in their next fight this turn[cite: 6].
* **"This is no longer an abstract experiment"** — All robots may move up to 2 areas this turn[cite: 6]. They may move witout being accompanied by humans.
* **"In the movies they say, Light them up!"** — Immediately eliminate 1 Green or Red Mutant from anywhere on the board[cite: 6].
* **"Most of these students failed my class"** — Professor Richardson fights with `CV: 3x2` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"GripBots have a good throwing arm"** — GripBots™ fight with `CV: 4x2` this turn[cite: 6].

### Professor Mincey Deck
* **"Woodstock Reset Time to Zero!"** — 1970s rock blares over the campus PA system[cite: 6]. All human players can reroll one die this turn. New result must be accepted.
* **"The Age of Aquarius is Upon us!"** — Ambient New Age synthesizer chimes echo through the corridors[cite: 6]. Professor Mincy CV becomes 3x2.
* **"Not to be bound by the rules…"** — Instantly teleport Dr. Mincey to any area inside the building[cite: 6].
* **"…but to create one’s own rules"** — Dr. Mincey’s Combat Value increases to `5` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Let’s test this frequency on the mutants"** — The Mutant player must immediately remove 1D6 mutants of their choice from play[cite: 6].

### Teaching Assistants Deck
* **"Intellectual Diversity Pledge"** — Buck fights with `CV: 5x2` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"College Founding Fathers Creed"** — Humans gain `+1 CV` in any combat involving Buck this turn[cite: 6].
* **"About that 2nd Amendment"** — GunBots™ gain `+1 CV` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Die you Hippy Mutant"** — Remove 1 mutant from the reserve line in Buck's next fight[cite: 6].
* **"Artificial Intelligence, Real Stupidity"** — Robot systems fault; all robots suffer `-1 CV` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"Zombie Attack Nerd Rage"** — Percy fights with `CV: 4x2` this turn[cite: 6].
* **"My elongated Oblongata is very sexy"** — 1 wounded robot is automatically repaired at the end of the turn[cite: 6].
* **"I turn coffee into theorems"** — All wounded robots on the board are instantly repaired[cite: 6].
* **"Logic and Set Theory"** — All mutants suffer `-1 CV` to defense in the next fight[cite: 6].
* **"Increase the gain on the controllers now!"** — Instantly reposition 1 robot to any area on the board[cite: 6].

### Laboratory Technology Bonus ###
* **Ad Astra Lab** - If the humans escape the Science Building through the Ad Astra portal at the end of the game, they enter Doctor Manbender's lab when he only has 3 Campus Security Mutants.
* **Quantum Physics Lab** - The Ad Astra device is warming up. A human or robot can exit The Tech Room and appear anywhere else in the Science Building.
* **Robotics Lab** - Robots are easier to repair, add +1 to Robot Repair for all charactes. Characters with N/A for Robotics Repair now have a 6+ Robot Repair.
* **High Performance Computer Lab** The door lock bug is now fixed. Mutants can only pass through smashed doors. Regular doors do not open when mutants are on both sides of it.
