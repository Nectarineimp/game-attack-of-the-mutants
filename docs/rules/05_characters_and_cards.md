## Event Card Deck

Draw 1 card during Phase 1. The effect lasts **only for the current turn** and only applies if the named character is currently in play.

### Professor Applewhite Deck
* **"Maybe they can be reasoned with!"** — Mutants gain `+1 CV` in the first combat this turn.
* **"Save your ammo, bullets can’t hurt them!"** — Humans suffer `-1 CV` in the first combat this turn.
* **"Robots should not have military use."** — GripBots™ may drag up to 2 wounded elements during retreats this turn. Elements are humans or bots.
* **"If my calculations are right…"** — GripBots™ gain `+1 CV` this turn.
* **"Grant money will flow if we survive!"** — Optionally fight 1 additional combat round in an engagement this turn.

### Professor Richardson Deck
* **"Here’s your education…"** — All robots gain `+1 CV` in their next fight this turn.
* **"Dance, Mutant, Dance!"** — All GunBots™ roll `3x3` CV in their next fight this turn.
* **"This is no longer an abstract experiment"** — All robots may move up to 2 areas this turn. They may move witout being accompanied by humans.
* **"In the movies they say, Light them up!"** — Immediately eliminate 1 Green or Red Mutant from anywhere on the board.
* **"Most of these students failed my class"** — Professor Richardson fights with `CV: 3x2` this turn.
* **"GripBots have a good throwing arm"** — GripBots™ fight with `CV: 4x2` this turn.

### Professor Mincey Deck
* **"Woodstock Reset Time to Zero!"** — 1970s rock blares over the campus PA system. All human players can reroll one die this turn. New result must be accepted.
* **"The Age of Aquarius is Upon us!"** — Ambient New Age synthesizer chimes echo through the corridors. Professor Mincy CV becomes 3x2.
* **"Not to be bound by the rules…"** — Instantly teleport Dr. Mincey to any area inside the building.
* **"…but to create one’s own rules"** — Dr. Mincey’s Combat Value increases to `5` this turn.
* **"Let’s test this frequency on the mutants"** — The Mutant player must immediately remove 1D6 mutants of their choice from play.

### Teaching Assistants Deck
* **"Intellectual Diversity Pledge"** — Buck fights with `CV: 5x2` this turn.
* **"College Founding Fathers Creed"** — Humans gain `+1 CV` in any combat involving Buck this turn.
* **"About that 2nd Amendment"** — GunBots™ gain `+1 CV` this turn.
* **"Die you Hippy Mutant"** — Remove 1 mutant from the reserve line in Buck's next fight.
* **"Artificial Intelligence, Real Stupidity"** — Robot systems fault; all robots suffer `-1 CV` this turn.
* **"Zombie Attack Nerd Rage"** — Percy fights with `CV: 4x2` this turn.
* **"My elongated Oblongata is very sexy"** — 1 wounded robot is automatically repaired at the end of the turn.
* **"I turn coffee into theorems"** — All wounded robots on the board are instantly repaired.
* **"Logic and Set Theory"** — All mutants suffer `-1 CV` to defense in the next fight.
* **"Increase the gain on the controllers now!"** — Instantly reposition 1 robot to any area on the board.

### Laboratory Technology Bonus ###
* **Ad Astra Lab** - If the humans escape the Science Building through the Ad Astra portal at the end of the game, they enter Doctor Manbender's lab when he only has 3 Campus Security Mutants.
* **Quantum Physics Lab** - The Ad Astra device is warming up. A human or robot can exit The Tech Room and appear anywhere else in the Science Building.
* **Robotics Lab** - Robots are easier to repair, add +1 to Robot Repair for all charactes. Characters with N/A for Robotics Repair now have a 6+ Robot Repair.
* **High Performance Computer Lab** The door lock bug is now fixed. Mutants can only pass through smashed doors. Regular doors do not open when mutants are on both sides of it.
