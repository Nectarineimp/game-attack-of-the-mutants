# Attack of the Mutants (Miniatures Edition)

## Overview & Narrative Background
Two hours ago, the Middle State Technical University (MSTU) campus was plunged into madness[cite: 3]. Professor Julian Manbender—convinced of his own supreme intellect and determined to forge a compliant, elevated society—detonated a mutagenic weapon at his free "Green Energy" rock concert[cite: 3]. Rather than clean energy, the device unleashed a biochemical agent that transformed the student body into a ravenous, snack-seeking mutant horde under his telepathic sway[cite: 3].

A first assault on the Science Building was narrowly repelled[cite: 3]. Now, a larger and hungrier horde surrounds the faculty and students inside[cite: 3]. The defenders must buy time to activate the experimental **Ad Astra** time rift and confront Manbender before the mutation cascade becomes permanent[cite: 3, 4].

---

## Game Components
To play the miniatures edition, you will need:
* **The Game Map:** Representing the Science Building interior, research labs, corridors, and exterior perimeter staging areas[cite: 4].
* **Miniatures:**
  * **Humans:** 3 Professors (Applewhite, Richardson, Mincey), 3 Teaching Assistants (Penny, Buck, Percy)[cite: 3, 4].
  * **Robots:** Semi-autonomous GunBots™ and GripBots™[cite: 3].
  * **Mutants:** Professor Julian Manbender, 5 Mutant Security Officers, 40 Red "Extra Glowing" Mutants, and 56 Green Standard Mutants[cite: 3, 4].
* **Markers & Tokens:** 12 Barricades, smashed door counters, and combat condition markers[cite: 4].
* **Cards & Dice:** The 10-turn Event Card Deck and a handful of standard six-sided dice (D6)[cite: 3, 4].
* **Tactical Combat Pad:** A staging pad dividing front line, reserves, and wounded casualties[cite: 3, 10].

## Victory Conditions

### Mutant Victory
* The mutants win immediately if they establish sole control of the **Ad Astra Lab** with no living humans present, destroying the mechanism[cite: 4].

### Human Victory
* The humans win by holding the **Ad Astra Lab** at the end of **Turn 10** (or at the end of **Turn 9** if Professor Mincey has occupied the lab continuously from Turn 6 through Turn 9)[cite: 3, 4].
* Holding the lab triggers the **Time Rift Assault**: all surviving humans and robots in the Ad Astra lab travel back to Dr. Julian Manbender's facility for a final showdown against Manbender and his 5 Mutant Campus Police[cite: 3, 4]. If Dr. Manbender is killed in this final battle, the time loop is broken and humanity is saved[cite: 3, 4]!
