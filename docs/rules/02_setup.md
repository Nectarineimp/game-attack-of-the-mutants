# Initial Setup

## Map Layout
Place the Science Building map in the center of the table[cite: 4]. Keep smashed door counters, casualty markers, and the 12 barricades nearby[cite: 4]. All mutants start outside the perimeter, while all humans and robots begin inside[cite: 4].

---

## Human Setup

### 1. Professor Placement & Initial Confinement
The Human player must place the three professors in three different labs selected from the four research facilities[cite: 4]:
* **Quantum Engineering Lab**
* **Robotics Lab**
* **Ad Astra Lab**
* **High Performance Computing Center**

> **Professor Lockdown Rule:**
> * All three professors must remain in their starting labs for the first three game turns (Turns 1–3)[cite: 4].
> * At the start of Turn 4, professors may move normally[cite: 4].
> * **Tech Upgrade Roll:** At the start of Turn 4, roll 1D6 for each lab occupied by a professor[cite: 4]. On a roll of **5 or 6**, that facility produces a breakthrough tech upgrade for the human defenders[cite: 4].

### 2. Teaching Assistants (TAs)
* Penny Applewhite, Leon "Buck" Bukaw, and Percy Fitzwater may be placed in **any room or corridor** inside the building[cite: 4].

### 3. Robots
* Robots (GripBots and GunBots) must start in an area occupied by at least one human, with one exception[cite: 3, 4]:
  * **GunBots™** may be deployed alone in hallways to set up passage choke points using their *Wall of Lead* ability[cite: 3, 4].

### 4. Barricade Placement
The human player places 12 physical barricades across doorways or hallway corridors subject to the following structural restrictions[cite: 4]:
* **Adjacency Limit:** Following the wall line from any barricade, it can only be adjacent to **at most one** other barricade[cite: 4]. The very next opening along that wall circuit must be an unbarricaded door[cite: 4].
* **Hallway Barricades:** A barricade built across a corridor counts simultaneously as both a wall and a barricade[cite: 4].
* **Impassable to Humans:** Humans cannot move through barricades and cannot smash them down (unlike standard automatic doors, which open freely for humans)[cite: 3, 4].

## Mutant Setup

### Exterior Staging Areas
* Mutants set up in the designated exterior staging areas surrounding the facility[cite: 4].
* **Capacity:** Each staging zone accommodates up to **2 mutants**[cite: 4].
* **Reserve Waves:** Mutants that do not fit in the starting staging areas remain in the exterior reserve pool and enter during subsequent turns[cite: 4].
* The Mutant player may select any combination of available mutant types to fill the staging positions[cite: 4].
