# Quick Reference Sheet (QRS)

## Turn Sequence

1. **Phase 1: Event Card Draw, Mutant Reinforcements**
   * Place Mutant reinforcements in exterior staging areas (max 2 per area)[cite: 4].
   * Draw 1 Event Card (active for this turn only; requires character in play)[cite: 4, 5].
   * Penny Applewhite chooses favorite: **Buck** (+1 CV) or **Percy** (+1 Repair)[cite: 4].
2. **Phase 2: Mutant Horde**
   * **Door / Barricade Smash Tests:** Roll on the Smash! Matrix (doors auto-open if mutants occupy both sides)[cite: 4].
   * **Movement:** Mutants move 1 area inward or shift between adjacent exterior staging zones[cite: 4].
   * **Mark Combats:** Flag all contested rooms/corridors[cite: 4].
3. **Phase 3: Human Movement & Repair**
   * Standard movement: 1 area (doors open freely; barricades cannot be crossed or smashed)[cite: 4].
   * **Buck’s Blitz:** Moves 2 areas if the first is clear; may bring humans/bots starting or picked up on step 1[cite: 4].
   * **Repairs:** Eligible humans/GripBots roll repair checks on damaged robots[cite: 4].
4. **Phase 4: Tactical Combat**
   * Mutant player chooses combat order[cite: 4]. Maximum 2 rounds per area[cite: 4].
   * Line max: 3 figures per side; outnumbering on the line grants **+1 CV** (negated by GunBot)[cite: 4, 5].
   * Attacker rolls 1D6 + CV; Defender rolls 1D6 + CV. Highest score wins; attacker wins ties (GunBot Wall of Lead counts as attacker)[cite: 4].
   * Wounded human/bot recovery: 1 unwounded figure in reserve or adjacent safe room pulls 1 wounded figure to safety[cite: 4].
   * Pressing Advantage: Victorious Humans may move 1 area forward; Victorious Mutants get 1 free Smash test[cite: 4].
5. **Phase 5: Victory Check**
   * Check Ad Astra Lab control; advance turn track[cite: 4].
